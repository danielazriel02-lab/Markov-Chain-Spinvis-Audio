LIE TO ME (Chris Isaak) - MARKOV TOKENS, ALL TRACKS
===================================================
Format (same as marioTokens in the Mario Jazz tutorial):
  "A3:04"          = note A3, lasting 4 sixteenths
  "ZZ:04"          = rest of 4 sixteenths
  "E3+C4+C#4:04"   = CHORD: these notes together (needs the chord change in the sketch)

Already adjusted to fit the Wicked Game tokens:
  - KEY: moved up 2 semitones, from G minor to A minor (same notes as Wicked Game)
  - RHYTHM: note lengths doubled, because this file is written at 112 BPM in normal notation
    and Wicked Game at 240 (double-speed notation). Now one beat means the same in both.
    So play these at the same tempo as Wicked Game (240, or your slower 180-200).
Repetition removed: repeated 4-bar blocks and long silences (4+ bars) are taken out.
All tracks in this file are guitars (the MIDI has no bass, pad, drums or vocals).

[*] = recommended to combine with Wicked Game
[SINGLE] = single notes, works in the current sketch
[CHORDS] = contains chord tokens, needs the chord change in the sketch first

Not included: clean 2 (exact copy of clean 1).
